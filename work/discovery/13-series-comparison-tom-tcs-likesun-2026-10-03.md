# Series comparison — TOM, TCS/TCSQ, Like the Sun

**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Status:** comparative extraction; series remain distinct

## Core comparison

| Dimension | TOM series | TCS / TCSQ | Like the Sun |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary vehicle | fable, field machine, layered story sequence | board, tile, route, dossier, reader choice | documentary-literary manuscript, letters, messages, memory, appendix |
| Reader position | watchdog, carrier, tester, possible misreader | route-maker and investigator | witness who must hold contradictory time-states without re-entry |
| Main pressure | order, repair, drift, mutilation, inheritance | evidence contamination, false closure, gap, route mutation | intimacy, boundary, memory, access, responsibility |
| Dominant transformation | sequence changes prior meaning | route changes report and motive | analytic map becomes embodied scene and later re-entry |
| Omission function | missing branch or payload threatens the cargo | gap actively routes the reader | omitted material returns as appendix, insertion, archive, or unresolved residue |
| Failure mode | readable fable with no load-bearing test | clean route that removes gap and ambiguity | coherent memoir that turns into verdict, diagnosis, or access claim |
| Control interface | order register, cargo test, invariant, repair ledger | routing matrix, trap/callback/mutation, reader/review modes | provenance layers, version comparison, privacy/legal gate, distance architecture |

## What they share

All three series treat form as operational. A change of order, route, layer,
or narrative distance changes what the reader can know and what the material can
carry. In each series, preservation is not identical to keeping the visible
artifact:

- TOM preserves cargo/function through order and repair.
- TCS preserves pressure through route, gap, and altered return.
- Like the Sun preserves temporal and ethical complexity through provenance,
  distance, versioning, and controlled re-entry.

## What they do not share

TOM is primarily a training machine: the reader is made capable of detecting and
carrying a mechanism.

TCS is primarily an adversarial interface: the reader’s route is part of the
evidence and can mutate the report.

Like the Sun is primarily a memory-and-boundary machine: the reader must keep
beauty, harm, contradiction, and the right to end contact in the same record.

TOM can abstract a mechanism into a fable. TCS can turn the mechanism into a
choice architecture. Like the Sun must protect recognizable human material from
becoming a weapon while still refusing to erase what happened.

## Shared invariant, different implementations

> A payload survives only when the receiving surface preserves its function,
> its cost, and the uncertainty that belongs to it.

| Series | Cost that must remain visible | Uncertainty that must remain visible |
|---|---|---|
| TOM | reader effort, repair, selection, carrier loss | whether the reader barked at the right rope |
| TCS/TCSQ | route choice, gap, return, contaminated report | which interpretation owns the evidence |
| Like the Sun | intimacy, access, memory, privacy, boundary | what a source proves versus what a later reading infers |

## Transfer verdict

The series are related by a transversal engine, not by a common genre or a
single plot machine. The safest architecture is:

```text
shared transfer invariant
  ├── TOM adapter: order / cargo / repair
  ├── TCS adapter: route / gap / altered return
  └── Like-the-Sun adapter: provenance / distance / re-entry
```

Do not merge the adapters. The same word — archive, gap, order, distance,
repair — carries different obligations in each series.
