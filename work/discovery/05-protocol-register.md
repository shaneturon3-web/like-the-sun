# Protocol register — v0.1

## Protocol used so far

- source-first recovery;
- exact Drive/GitHub inventory;
- low-resolution scan before deep reading;
- separate source, derivative, inference, and missing evidence;
- preservation of originals and labeled working extractions;
- reconciliation before deduplication;
- cross-corpus comparison without canon merge;
- resolution ladder from corpus to mechanism;
- superposition of competing volume assignments;
- object-family tracing across books;
- function test before symbolic interpretation;
- versioned records committed to GitHub;
- production deferred until a mechanism survives independent transfer.

## Delegation contract

A delegated GPT must use `BOOTSTRAP_CROSS_CORPUS_RECOVERY.md` as its entry instruction and return evidence only. It may extend the inventory and propose hypotheses, but may not write manuscript material, repair missing scenes, or collapse the three-book corpus into one undifferentiated work.
