## Cross-surface invariant under test

> Preserve the functional payload while changing the carrier, route, register,
> or volume — and expose the loss when the transfer cannot preserve it.

| Surface | Carrier of the function | Failure if flattened |
|---|---|---|
| TOM1 | order, cargo, reader test | reader receives a lesson without the load-bearing sequence |
| TOM2 | layer, wrong lesson, selection | archive becomes cleanup; adversarial pressure disappears |
| Companion | register, matrix, rotated route | explanation replaces active mechanism |
| Tactical | charge, scar, missing branch | compression becomes amputation or a dead manual |

## Result

The four surfaces confirm a common transfer invariant, but they do not collapse
into one book or one skill. The same function is carried by different operators:

- TOM1: order changes interpretation;
- TOM2: selection changes future capacity;
- Companion: rotation tests survival across registers;
- Tactical: cuts leave scars and return paths.

This is sufficient for a **plugin-level candidate grouping**, not yet for a
single formal skill. Promotion still requires an adversarial test in which one
surface is deliberately stripped of its carrier and the ledger checks whether
the payload survives or fails.
