# Bootstrap — Cross-Corpus Recovery & Cognitive Extraction

Version: v0.1
Date: 2026-10-03
Purpose: resume source-first research across the three-book corpus without collapsing distinct books, volumes, voices, or evidence classes.

## Corpus scope

Treat these as one comparative corpus with separate surfaces:

1. `like-the-sun`
2. `pre-like-the-sun`
3. `El niño que vino del mar`

Do not merge their canon, characters, scenes, or repositories. Compare mechanisms and transfers explicitly.

Internal volume hypotheses such as `Vanilla`, `Infierno`, and `Diez años de silencio` remain provisional and must not redefine the corpus.

## Operating posture

Research first. Do not draft, repair, normalize, publish, or promote canon during discovery.

Use four evidence labels:

- **VERIFIED SOURCE** — directly read from Drive, GitHub, or a supplied chat/source.
- **DERIVED RECORD** — an extracted index, map, summary, or generated derivative.
- **INFERENCE** — a mechanism or relation supported by multiple observations.
- **UNRESOLVED / MISSING EVIDENCE** — plausible but not established.

Chat history is reader-response and process evidence unless a primary source is independently recovered. Never treat a chat summary as manuscript canon.

## Resolution ladder

Move deliberately between:

`corpus → book → volume → chapter → scene → object → mechanism → transfer`

At every change of resolution, preserve the lower-level evidence. Do not replace a scene with its mechanism or an object with its symbolic interpretation.

## Superposition rule

Keep competing readings active until a source resolves them. Record:

- object;
- local function;
- scene pressure;
- candidate book/volume;
- evidence source;
- confidence class;
- unresolved alternatives.

Repeated objects do not automatically have repeated meanings.

## Extraction protocol

1. Inventory exact titles, URLs, IDs, branches, commits, dates, and formats.
2. Read short indexes and entry documents before long corpora.
3. Preserve originals; create labeled working extractions.
4. Compare actual text before calling items duplicates or revisions.
5. Scan at low resolution for structure and rare mechanisms.
6. Change resolution only when a repeated relation appears.
7. Test a mechanism across at least two surfaces before calling it transversal.
8. Record what is absent, inaccessible, contradictory, or only inferred.
9. Write a bounded research record before any production artifact.
10. Commit and push the record to the designated branch.

## Current corpus mechanism under test

`initial reading → material/relational test → function outranks interpretation → memory is reclassified`

Observed provisionally in:

- Like the Sun: affection becomes safe distance;
- Pre-like the Sun: perfect chair becomes lived family object;
- NINO: incomplete trace becomes temporary function before explanation.

Do not promote this to corpus law until another independent scene supports it.

## Chat-pesquisa protocol

For chats unavailable in the current environment:

1. Search exact title, conversation ID, distinctive phrase, and project name.
2. If a chat is accessible, read bounded recent turns first and follow cursors only as needed.
3. Treat user corrections as high-value process evidence but verify claims against source files.
4. Extract mechanisms, source pointers, corrections, and unresolved questions.
5. Do not recreate missing chapters from memory.
6. Report `not found in searched scope`, never `does not exist`.

## Required output from a delegated researcher

Return a compact evidence packet containing:

- searched locations and queries;
- accessible chats and source IDs;
- verified mechanisms with exact anchors;
- competing interpretations;
- missing evidence and access failures;
- candidate transfers between the three books;
- recommended next fetches;
- no prose production and no canon promotion.
